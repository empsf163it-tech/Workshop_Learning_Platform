Adaptive Learning Levels

Explore how AbilityLearn tailors curriculum complexity to match individual developmental stages.

Level 1

Foundational Exploration

Multi-sensory activities focused on core visual recognition, audio prompts, and basic single-touch interaction tasks.

  • High-contrast visual cues
  • Audio narration for every step
  • Zero timer pressure
Level 2

Guided Practice

Interactive lessons breaking literacy, math, and life skills into bite-sized sequential tasks with instant positive reinforcement.

  • Customizable reading speeds
  • Step-by-step visual hints
  • Caregiver progress markers
Level 3

Independent Mastery

Self-paced module goals encouraging autonomous problem-solving with optional assistive overlays and focus modes.

  • Adaptive difficulty scaling
  • Focus-masking overlays
  • Self-reflection prompts
