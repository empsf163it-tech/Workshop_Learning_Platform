About AbilityLearn

Inclusive education starts with making space for different needs.

AbilityLearn is a website concept for an inclusive learning platform centered on accessible lessons, personalized pathways, assistive tools, and shared learner support.

Our approach

Design around participation, not limitations.

The AbilityLearn experience is designed to help learners engage with educational content in ways that feel clear, respectful, and practical.

Every learner can benefit from education that is easier to access, easier to understand, and flexible enough to meet different needs.

Platform values

What shapes AbilityLearn

I

Inclusion

Create learning experiences where different abilities and learning preferences are considered from the start.

C

Clarity

Keep navigation, content, and progress information understandable and purposeful.

F

Flexibility

Offer choices in pacing, presentation, and support so learning can adapt to the person.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick responses to common questions about platform access, support, and partnerships.

How can schools integrate AbilityLearn?

AbilityLearn offers institutional onboarding for classrooms and district networks, providing custom learning path setup and teacher dashboards.

Is AbilityLearn free for individual caregivers?

Yes, core learning pathways and essential assistive tools are freely accessible to individual learners and caregivers.

What assistive technology hardware is supported?

AbilityLearn is browser-based and compatible with standard screen readers, switch access devices, eye-tracking input, and tablet touchscreens.

How do I request a custom feature for my child or student?

You can contact our research & engineering team directly through the contact form above with specific accessibility requests.
